The corpus

79 distinct ingredients appear across 8 eyeliners, and 51 of them (64%) appear in exactly one product. Not one ingredient reaches a 75% share of the corpus — the common spine that foundation has (7 ingredients in three-quarters of the corpus) does not exist here.

Eyeliner carries no SPF claim, so unlike foundation it is not regulated as an OTC drug in the United States and files nothing with the FDA. Every ingredient list here instead comes from the manufacturer's own official product page — still a first-party declaration, legally required under the FPLA labeling law, just not a government filing. Retailer listings (Sephora, Ulta, Amazon) are not used as sources, same standard as the foundation corpus.

Two vehicles, one category

5 of these 8 are liquid liners (water-based); 3 are pencil or kohl liners (wax-based, no water at all). Foundation's corpus is one vehicle — liquid, lotion, cream or emulsion — so its formulas share a real spine. Eyeliner's does not: a wax pencil and a water-based liquid liner are built from different chemistry to do the same job on the same part of the face, so "eyeliner" turns out to be two categories wearing one name.
