What's in my makeup?

Twelve foundations compared ingredient by ingredient, with a slider to put any two side by side. 191 distinct ingredients; only 7 appear in three-quarters of them.

Source: FDA-filed OTC drug labels (DailyMed)

Is it really better?

Does a dearer formula mean rarer chemistry? Measured: price buys a longer list and better-placed actives, but ingredient rarity is flat across every tier.

Source: ingredient corpus · label ordering rule

What am I paying for?

e.l.f. runs a 70.7% gross margin; Estée Lauder runs 74.0%. The formula in a $53 bottle costs about what the formula in a $12 bottle costs.

Source: SEC 10-K · company annual reports

Was it sourced ethically?

An ingredient list names what a substance is and never where it came from. L'Oréal's own RSPO filing shows 99.5% of its certified palm is mass balance — an accounting claim, not a traceable molecule.

Source: RSPO Annual Communication of Progress · scheme registries

Who really makes it?

A $19 L'Oréal Paris foundation and a $53 Lancôme one declare the same first nine ingredients in the same order. Both brands belong to L'Oréal.

Source: FDA filings · permutation test

What's banned where?

The EU prohibits 1,760 substances in cosmetics and the US prohibits nine. Not one of the 191 ingredients in this corpus appears on either list — the real divergence is elsewhere.

Source: EU Cosmetics Regulation · 21 CFR 700 · REACH

Who's watching?

The first substantial change to US cosmetics law since 1938, enacted three and a half years ago. Every requirement that needs FDA to write a rule is still outstanding.

Source: Public Law 117-328 · Federal Register

How long does it take?

No manufacturer discloses how many prototypes a formula takes — so this sources what actually is public: the stability testing a formula must pass before it can ship.

Source: 21 CFR 211.166 · EU Decision 2013/674/EU

Does R&D spend buy quality?

Half the parent companies behind this corpus disclose no R&D figure at all. Among the three that do, spending more does not visibly buy a cleaner or more concentrated formula.

Source: SEC 10-K · company annual reports

Is the talc gone?

Every SPF setting powder with an FDA filing is talc-free — they're mineral sunscreens, a different tradition entirely. The brands actually reformulating away from talc carry no SPF claim, so this page sources both halves separately.

Source: FDA-filed OTC drug labels · FDA asbestos testing
