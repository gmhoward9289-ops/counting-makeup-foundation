## What this site is

This site applies a statistical/research model — the same approach used by its
sibling project, counting-chicken-wings — to cosmetics: ingredient comparisons,
"is it really better" style analysis, estimated cost-to-make versus retail
pricing, and sourcing/ethics claims. All figures are derived estimates based on
publicly available information, not verified manufacturer data.
