How To Use It

  • For a thicker line or cat-eye effect, follow up with a second, shorter line slightly above the first (just in the last third of your lashes), then color in the empty space.
  • PRO TIP: If you're afraid of messing up, try dabbing on dots along your upper lashes, then slowly connecting them to make the line.
  • PAIR IT: For a complete eye look, sweep Revlon Glimmer Shadow Multi-Stick across your lids before lining — the cream-to-powder formula sets fast, so it won't smudge under your liner.
