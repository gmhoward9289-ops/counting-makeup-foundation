PRODUCT DETAILS
Haute Precision Liquid Eyeliner by L’Oréal Paris features an ultra-fine 0.01mm cushioned felt tip for precise, effortless, fade-defying color that lasts. Its silky ink formula glides on smoothly to create intense and sharp eye looks with stunning definition. The long-lasting waterproof formula is fade-proof and transfer-resistant for up to 28 hours. Available in five versatile shades, Haute Precision Liquid Eyeliner is the ultimate tool for achieving bold wings, defined lines, and statement-making graphic eye looks with ease.

Product Highlights
  • Ultra-fine 0.01mm cushioned felt tip: Achieve smooth, precise lines with ease.
  • Long-lasting wear: Stays vibrant and smudge-resistant for up to 28 hours.
  • Waterproof formula: Resists smudging, fading, and transferring, even through humidity or tears.
  • Intense color payoff: Silky, highly pigmented formula for bold or subtle looks.
  • Five versatile shades: Perfect for classic and bold eye makeup designs.

Why Choose Haute Precision Liquid Eyeliner?

This waterproof liquid eyeliner provides definition with its ultra-fine micro-tip design, creating clean, sharp lines with every stroke. The waterproof, fade-proof, and transfer-resistant formula ensures vibrant and bold pigmentation that lasts up to 28 hours, making it perfect for any occasion. With five versatile shades, this eyeliner adapts to your personal style, from classic to bold, with smooth, fade-proof application every time. Whether you're aiming for a sleek winged liner or intricate graphic designs, Haute Precision excels in precision and color payoff.
WHO SHOULD USE
Best for:
  • Anyone seeking precise, sharp eyeliner looks or defined eye makeup styles.
  • People who want long-lasting waterproof liquid eyeliner that stays vibrant all day without smudging or fading.
  • Those who prefer an easy-to-use liquid eyeliner with a smooth, lightweight formula.
HOW TO PICK YOUR SHADE

Explore the Haute Precision Liquid Eyeliner collection, featuring five versatile shades perfect for any eye look:
  • Black Silk
  • Brown Leather
  • Blue Suede
  • Burgundy Cashmere
  • Green Velvet
Use the tips below to find your ideal shade:
  1. Check your style needs:
    • Use Black Silk for bold, classic definition.
    • Select Brown Leather for a softer, everyday look.
    • Try Blue Suede, Burgundy Cashmere, or Green Velvet to enhance your eye makeup with unique, vibrant shades.
  2. Experiment with different looks:
    • Dark shades like Black Silk and Brown Leather are perfect for sharp lines and defined wings.
    • Add a pop of color with Blue Suede, Burgundy Cashmere, or Green Velvet for a striking twist to your style.
Pro Tip: Use the Virtual Try-On Tool to test these shades and discover the one that complements your style, whether you're perfecting a sharp liner or dramatic smoky eye makeup.
HOW TO USE

Step 1: Apply.
Glide the liquid eyeliner along your upper lash line, starting from the inner corner and moving outward using the 0.01mm ultra-fine micro-tip for precise application.

Step 2: Define.
For winged or graphic eyeliner looks, use the micro-tip to create sharp, defined lines or extend outward to achieve dramatic looks.

Step 3: Perfect.
Allow the formula to dry naturally for long-lasting, smudge-resistant, and waterproof wear. Reapply as desired for an even bolder finish.

Step 4: Enhance.
To complete your look, complement this liquid eyeliner with a long-lasting eyeshadow (like Paradise Le Shadow Stick) and a chromatic mascara like Voluminous Panorama Waterproof Mascara.
