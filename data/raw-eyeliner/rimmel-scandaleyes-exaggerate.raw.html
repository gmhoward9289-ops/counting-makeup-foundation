Scandaleyes Exaggerate Liquid Eyeliner

001 Black Select your shade/ 1
Rimmel London Scandaleyes Exaggerate Eyeliner is a highly pigemented liquid eyeliner that delivers a long-lasting, bold black look. Use the intense color to create prestige looks or a classic feline flick that lasts up to 16 hours. The smooth color formula glides on easily with the easy-to-use makeup applicator tool to ensure maximum control for creating precise eye looks. It's smudge-proof, flake-proof, smear-proof and run-proof. Dermatologist & Opthamologist tested. Live the London look!

How to apply

Apply the liner starting at the inside corner of your eye and work outwards. For a bold cat-eye, go beyond the outer corner of your eye.
Aqua/Water/Eau, Propylene Glycol, Ammonium Acrylates Copolymer, Butylene Glycol, Phenoxyethanol, Magnesium Aluminum Silicate, Cellulose Gum, Talc, Panthenol, Simethicone, Pantolactone, [May Contain/Peut Contenir/+/-: Iron Oxides (CI 77491, CI 77492, CI 77499), Black 2 [Nano]].
